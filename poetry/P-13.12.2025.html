Poetry

For my sister

Published: 13th December 2025

I don’t say this enough… but you are my best friend.
And I don’t mean the kind of best friend who just laughs with you
Or shares snacks.
I mean the kind of best friend who has held my heart
And I have held yours.
Through every storm.
Every silence.
Every moment that should have broken us.

We shared ten years under the same roof.
Ten years of quiet nights.
Whispers in the dark.
Me holding you close
While the thunder shook the windows
And you were too small to stop trembling.
I told you you were safe
Even when I wasn’t sure myself.
I had to tell you… because someone had to.

I was nineteen.
You were ten.
And then everything changed.
You went to live with your Dad.
And Mum —
Mum tried to leave us.
She tried to disappear.
And suddenly…
The house wasn’t ours anymore.
It was just me.
Me and the echoes.
Me and the quiet that felt too loud.

I lived alone in that house after you left.
Every room reminded me of you.
Every shadow reminded me of fear.
And I realized…
I was still the one who had to keep holding on
Even when it felt like no one else could.

People talk about that year… like it’s over.
Like it ended.
But it didn’t.
It lives in the way we love.
In the way we worry.
In the way we still check on each other.
It lives in the way we remember
What we almost lost.

Even after we lived apart —
Different houses.
Different rooms.
Different worlds.
I never felt that you were gone.
Because even across the distance
We carried the same memories.
The same heartbeat
That said… we are still here.

I think about those nights sometimes.
How I stayed awake to keep you safe.
And how the silence of the house after you left
Taught me how to survive alone.
How to be brave when no one else was watching.
And even though I wasn’t there with you
I felt you with me.
Always.

You know me in ways no one else ever will.
I see you in ways the world can’t touch.
I remember the little girl trembling under storms
And I see the woman you’ve become:
Brave.
Strong.
Tender.
The same softness I held in my arms —
But unbreakable.

We didn’t get a soft childhood.
We didn’t get safety or certainty.
We got each other.
And even across the distance
Even with different lives
Even with years between us
That has always been enough.

I carry you with me.
In quiet moments.
In loud moments.
In the small soft parts of my heart
That remember how we survived.
How we held on.
How we stayed.

I want you to know this:
You are loved.
Completely.
Even when life tried to break us.
Even when it almost did.
Even after six years.
You are still my home.
My safe place.
My little sister.
I love you.
Always.
No matter the distance.
No matter the time.
No matter what comes next.
