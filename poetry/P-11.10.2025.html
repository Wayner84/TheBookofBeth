Poetry

Mine

Published: 11th October 2025

ADHD is in me.
It walks with me, it talks with me.
It’s not a stranger anymore —
it’s the voice that finishes my sentences
and forgets what we were saying halfway through.

My mind’s a room with every light on.
It’s bright, it’s buzzing,
it’s too much and never enough.

Every thought’s shouting to be heard,
and I can’t tell which one’s important
until it’s already gone.
I chase one down,
but it slips away —
a butterfly thought,
too fast,
too fragile,
too loud with colour to hold still.

Time’s a mess.
Five minutes can vanish,
or it can drag like wet cement.
Deadlines and dreams blur together
until I can’t tell which one I’m failing.

I live in almosts.
Almost done.
Almost there.
Almost the person I was meant to be today.

Some days it feels like chaos has a heartbeat
and it’s mine.
I can’t turn it off.
Can’t quiet the noise.
Can’t stop the part of me that starts everything
and finishes nothing.

But sometimes,
in the middle of the storm,
there’s this spark —
a flash of something brilliant,
alive,
mine.
And for a second,
it all makes sense.
The noise,
the mess,
the motion —
it’s me.
And maybe that’s okay.

ADHD is in me.
It walks with me,
talks with me,
trips me,
pushes me,
moves me.
It’s not a curse.
It’s just the way my fire burns.
